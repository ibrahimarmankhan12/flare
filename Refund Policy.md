# **1. Purchases & Billing**

Flare AI may offer:

* Paid subscriptions
* One-time credit purchases
* Upgrades or add-ons
* API usage-based billing

All payments are processed securely through authorized payment providers.

---

# **2. Refund Eligibility**

We provide refunds under specific conditions. Refund approval is **not guaranteed** unless it meets one of the situations below:

### **2.1 Accidental or Duplicate Purchases**

You may request a refund if you:

* Purchased the same plan or credits twice
* Were charged more than once due to a billing error

### **2.2 Technical Issues**

Refunds may be approved if:

* The service was completely unusable
* A major outage prevented usage for an extended period
* You were unable to access paid features despite support attempts

### **2.3 Unauthorized Transactions**

If a payment was made without your permission, you must notify us immediately.
We may require verification before issuing a refund.

---

# **3. Non-Refundable Situations**

Refunds are **not** given for:

* Change of mind
* Unused time on subscription after cancellation
* Remaining unused credits
* Dissatisfaction with AI responses
* Temporary downtime or minor issues
* Misuse or violation of Terms of Service

All digital goods are considered delivered once added to your account.

---

# **4. Subscription Cancellations**

You may cancel your subscription at any time.

After cancellation:

* Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period.
* No future payments will be charged.
* No partial-period refunds are issued.

---

# **5. Refund Request Process**

To request a refund, contact us with:

* Your full name
* Email associated with your account
* Payment method
* Transaction ID or receipt
* Reason for the refund request

We aim to review all refund requests within **7 business days**.

---

# **6. Chargebacks**

If you initiate a chargeback:

* Your Flare AI account may be suspended
* Previous usage may be reviewed for violations
* We may dispute invalid chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a dispute — most issues can be solved quickly.
