# **1. What Are Cookies?**

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.
They help websites remember your actions, preferences, and login status.

Cookies may be:

* **Session cookies:** Removed when you close your browser
* **Persistent cookies:** Remain stored until they expire or are deleted
