## 4. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to deliver, improve, and secure our services. Here is a detailed breakdown:

---

### 4.1 Account & Identity Information

When you create a Flare account, we collect:

- **Full name** — to personalize your experience
- **Email address** — for account authentication, notifications, and support
- **Password** — stored in a securely hashed format (we never store plain-text passwords)
- **Profile photo** — optional; uploaded by you to personalize your account
- **Username or display name** — if applicable
- **Date of account creation**
- **Account type** (free, pro, or other tier)

---

### 4.2 Chat & Conversation Data

When you interact with any AI model on Flare:

- **Message content** — the text, prompts, and inputs you type or paste
- **AI responses** — the full responses generated by the AI
- **Conversation history** — saved so you can continue or reference past chats
- **Model selection** — which AI model you chose for a given conversation
- **System prompts or custom instructions** — if you configure them
- **Files or documents shared in chat** — if applicable
- **Timestamps** of each message

This data is stored to allow you to revisit your conversation history and for continuity across sessions.

---

### 4.3 Usage & Behavioral Data

We automatically collect data about how you use Flare:

- Pages and features visited within the app
- Buttons clicked, menus opened, and navigation paths
- Feature usage (e.g., how often you use image generation vs. voice mode)
- Search queries or topics you explore
- Time spent in sessions and individual features
- Error logs and crash reports
- API call frequency (for API users)

This data is used in **anonymized and aggregated form** to improve the product.

---

### 4.4 Device & Technical Data

When you access Flare, we automatically log:

- **IP address** — used for security, fraud prevention, and approximate geolocation
- **Browser type and version** (e.g., Chrome 120, Safari 17)
- **Operating system** (e.g., Windows 11, macOS Sonoma, Android 14)
- **Device type** (desktop, mobile, tablet)
- **Screen resolution**
- **Referring URL** (the page you came from before landing on Flare)
- **Language preferences**
- **Time zone**

---

### 4.5 Payment & Billing Information

If you subscribe to a paid Flare plan:

- Payment processing is handled by a **third-party payment provider** (e.g., Stripe or Razorpay)
- We do **not** store your full card number, CVV, or banking details on our servers
- We store only: subscription tier, billing date, payment status, and a tokenized reference from the payment provider

---

### 4.6 Communications Data

If you contact us via email, support chat, or feedback forms:

- Your name and email address
- The content of your message or request
- Any attachments or screenshots you share
- Our responses and resolution history

---

### 4.7 Voice & Audio Data

If you use Flare's Voice Mode feature:

- Your spoken audio is transcribed to text in real-time
- Raw audio is **not stored** on our servers after transcription
- The resulting text transcription may be stored as part of your conversation history

More details in [Section 19](#19-voice-mode--audio-data).

---

### 4.8 Image Generation Data

If you use Flare's image generation or editing tools:

- Your text prompts are stored temporarily to generate and display results
- Generated images may be cached briefly for performance
- Prompt history may be saved to your account for your reference

More details in [Section 20](#20-image-generation--editing-data).

## 11. Data Storage & Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement industry-standard practices:

### 11.1 Encryption
- All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using **TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS)**
- Sensitive data at rest (passwords, personal information) is encrypted using **AES-256** or equivalent
- Passwords are hashed using a secure one-way algorithm (e.g., bcrypt or Argon2)

### 11.2 Access Controls
- Access to user data is **restricted to authorized personnel only**, on a need-to-know basis
- Internal systems use role-based access control (RBAC)
- Administrative access requires multi-factor authentication (MFA)

### 11.3 Infrastructure Security
- Our infrastructure is hosted on reputable cloud providers with their own security certifications
- We conduct regular security reviews and vulnerability assessments
- We have incident response procedures in place

### 11.4 Breach Notification
In the event of a data breach that compromises your personal information, we will:
- Notify affected users within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach
- Notify relevant regulatory authorities as required by applicable law
- Provide clear guidance on steps you can take to protect yourself

### 11.5 Limitations
No method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we do our best to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and enable any available account security features.

## 19. Voice Mode & Audio Data

Flare's Voice Mode allows you to speak to AI models using your microphone. Here's how we handle audio:

- **Microphone access** is requested only when you activate Voice Mode
- **Live audio** is processed in real-time for speech-to-text transcription

We do not build voice profiles, speaker identification systems, or biometric databases from your audio.

## 21. Model Context Protocol (MCP) & Data Connectors

Flare allows you to connect your external accounts (such as Google Drive) and local/remote datasets via the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and dedicated connectors.

Here is how we handle data related to these integrations:

- **Authentication & Tokens**: When you connect an external service (e.g., via Google OAuth) or configure an MCP server, we collect and securely store access tokens, connection strings, and authorization metadata. **Tokens are securely encrypted at rest (AES-256) and transmitted via TLS.**
- **Data Access & Usage**: We do not preemptively download, copy, or train models on your connected data. Data from Google Connectors or MCP servers is fetched **only on-demand** when you explicitly query or interact with it in a conversation.
- **Scope Limitations**: We only request the minimum permissions (scopes) necessary for the connector to function. Our access is strictly bound to generating AI responses based on your active prompts.
- **Token Deletion**: If you disconnect a service or delete your Flare account, your OAuth tokens and MCP connection strings are permanently securely deleted from our active database.
